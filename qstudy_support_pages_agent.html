Student registration
How do I register a student for an event?
Go to Events from your dashboard and select the event you want to register for. Click Register student, fill in all required fields — full name (as per ID), email, phone, preferred country, course interest, education qualification, and current city — then submit. You'll see the student appear in your registration list instantly.
What if a student doesn't have a passport yet?
Students can initially register with any government-issued ID. However, for Event Entry and Visa Processing, a valid passport is mandatory. They can update their passport details in the portal later once they receive it.
What documents does a student need to upload?
Required documents vary by event, but typically include:
  • Passport (mandatory for all events)
  • Academic transcripts (last qualification)
  • IELTS/TOEFL score card (if applying for English-speaking countries)
  • Any additional documents specified by the event
You can upload documents from the student's profile page under the Documents tab.
Can I register the same student for multiple events?
Yes, you can register a student for multiple events as long as the event dates don't clash and seats are available. The system will alert you if a duplicate registration is detected for the exact same event. Each event registration is tracked separately.
I see a "duplicate registration" warning — what do I do?
This means a student with the same email or phone has already been registered for this event — either by you or another agent. Check your student list first. If you believe it is an error, raise a Student-related support ticket and our team will investigate within 24 hours.
Events & seats
How do I know how many seats are left for an event?
Seat availability is shown in real-time on the Event Listings page. Each event card shows a seat count badge — green for open, amber when fewer than 20 seats remain. Once an event is full, registration closes automatically and you'll see a "Fully booked" label.
How can I filter events by country?
On the Events Dashboard, use the "Filter" button at the top right. You can filter events by Country, Date Range, and Institution type. This helps you find the most relevant opportunities for your students.
What happens if a student I registered does not attend?
If a student does not attend, their status will remain as Confirmed and will not progress to Attended. This may affect your commission eligibility for that student. Always follow up with students before the event to confirm attendance and reduce no-shows.
Will I be notified about new events?
Yes. When QStudy adds a new event, you'll receive a notification via email, SMS, and in-app. You can manage your notification preferences under Settings → Notifications. Make sure your email and phone number are up to date in your profile.
Invoices & payments
How do I raise an invoice?
Go to Invoices from your sidebar and click New invoice. Select the event, and the system will automatically pull the number of confirmed and converted students attributed to you. Review the amount, add any notes, and submit. The admin team reviews invoices within 3–5 business days.
My invoice was rejected — what should I do?
Check the rejection reason shown on the invoice detail page. Common reasons include student count mismatch, missing attendance confirmation, or incorrect event selected. Correct the issue and resubmit, or raise a Payment support ticket if you believe the rejection was incorrect.
How long does payment take after invoice approval?
Once your invoice is approved, payment is processed within 7–10 business days to the bank account configured in your payment settings. You will receive an email confirmation when the transfer is initiated. Make sure your payment details under Settings → Payment are correct to avoid delays.
Account & profile
How do I update my agency or contact details?
Go to Settings → Profile and update your name, agency name, phone number, address, or email. Some fields (like email) may require re-verification. Changes are saved immediately. If you need to update your verification documents, raise a support ticket under the Other category.
My account is showing "Pending verification" — what does that mean?
Pending verification means the QStudy admin team is reviewing your submitted documents (passport and agency registration). This process usually takes 1–2 business days. During this time you can browse events but cannot register students. You'll receive an email once your account is verified or if any documents are missing.
